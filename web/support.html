Moving to a new phone

On your old phone, open Settings (the sliders icon), tap Save code under "New phone?", and keep the code. On the new phone, tap Restore and paste it.

Restoring Daily Sort+

Open any locked puzzle or the Daily Sort+ page and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Cancelling a subscription

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Daily Sort+. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for. If you are on a free trial, cancelling before it ends means you are never charged.

Refunds
