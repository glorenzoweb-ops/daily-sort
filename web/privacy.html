Privacy Policy

Daily Sort · last updated November 23, 2026

Daily Sort is a puzzle game made by one person. It is built to need as little of your data as possible.

What stays on your device

Your progress, streak, stats, settings, friends' results and reactions are saved only on your phone (or in your browser on the web). There is no account and no sign-in. A backup code, if you make one, is created on your device, and you choose where to keep it.

What we don't collect

  • No ads and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts, photos or microphone.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or payment details. To know which purchases belong to you, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app ID and your purchase receipts from the store. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Sharing with friends

When you share a result, the link holds only what you see in the message: the puzzle number, your moves, tries, streak and the name you picked. It goes wherever you choose to send it.

Notifications

If you start a free trial, the app can remind you two days before it ends. This reminder is scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to a server.

Children

Daily Sort is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
